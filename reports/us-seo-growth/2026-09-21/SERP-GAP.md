# RoomFeng US SEO Growth 002 — Current US SERP Gap Review

Reviewed: 2026-09-21. Search results were used for intent and expectation research only; competitor wording, formulas, layouts, and visual designs were not copied.

## 1. `how to measure furniture for delivery`

- Visible result types: retailer measurement guides from [Pottery Barn](https://www.potterybarn.com/tips-and-ideas/how-to-measure-for-furniture/), [Crate & Barrel](https://www.crateandbarrel.com/ideas-and-advice/how-to-measure-furniture?msockid=0cf1286226c76a5c34c63ed0278a6b9c), [CB2](https://www.cb2.com/customer-service/how-to-measure), and [The Furniture Mart](https://www.thefurnituremart.com/inspiration/how-to-measure-furniture-for-delivery/).
- Common coverage: room footprint, furniture width/depth/height, diagonal depth, every doorway/hallway/stair/elevator checkpoint, fixtures and turns, and painter-tape visualization. Several results include diagrams or retailer help.
- RoomFeng gap: the existing page explained the topic generally but did not put the delivery-route answer first, did not provide a complete ordered checkpoint table, and did not separate assembled room fit from packaged/disassembled route fit.
- Added: direct answer, item-in-travel-form table, ordered route table, removable-parts dining-table example, three-fit-question handoff, and visible practical references.
- Intentionally not added: a guaranteed 3D fit claim, building-code thresholds, a new delivery calculator, or a new URL.

## 2. `will furniture fit through door elevator`

- Visible result types: retailer delivery guides, moving/delivery explainers, and user questions. The guides commonly cover door openings, hallways, stair landings, elevator doors and elevator cab dimensions; [The Furniture Mart guide](https://www.thefurnituremart.com/inspiration/how-to-measure-furniture-for-delivery/) explicitly distinguishes door and cab measurements and asks users to account for fixtures and landings.
- Common measurements/data: clear opening width/height, diagonal or angled screening measurements, approach space, turn space, stair headroom, landing width/depth, elevator door and cab dimensions.
- RoomFeng gap: the existing page did not name the full route as a sequence and could be read as a door/stair check rather than a route-planning guide for apartment delivery.
- Added: building entrance → lobby turn → hallway → doorway → stairs/landing → elevator → apartment door → final room checkpoint order, plus a clearly non-definitive 72 × 38 × 30 in table example.
- Intentionally not added: a route PASS/FAIL calculator or a claim that one diagonal comparison proves a turn.

## 3. `small bedroom layout with desk and wardrobe`

- Visible result types: interactive bedroom planners and editorial layout advice. Examples reviewed include [FloorPlanMaker](https://www.floorplanmaker.app/bedroom-layout-planner), [RoomFitPlanner bedroom layouts](https://www.roomfitplanner.com/bedroom-layouts), and [Designing Idea's planner](https://designingidea.com/free-bedroom-layout-planner/).
- Common coverage: bed-first placement, desk and wardrobe selection, room-size presets, drag-and-drop tools, and general clearance advice.
- RoomFeng gap: the existing page was useful but lacked a focused answer for the exact three-piece query and lacked transparent room-size arithmetic.
- Added: dedicated measured 8 × 10, 9 × 10, and 10 × 10 studies; one original `MeasuredPlan` diagram for 10 × 10; bed-frame caveat; planner CTA and handoff to the general small-bedroom workflow.
- Intentionally not added: generic decorating copy, universal walkway standards, or a new component/global CSS.

## 4. `small bedroom layout planner`

- Visible result types: free/interactive planners and bedroom layout collections such as [RoomFitPlanner](https://www.roomfitplanner.com/bedroom-layouts) and [FloorPlanMaker](https://www.floorplanmaker.app/bedroom-layout-planner).
- Common coverage: room-size templates, bed/storage/desk placement, drag-and-drop or to-scale previews, and export/share features.
- RoomFeng gap: the page needed a clearer map from general planning intent to the six existing exact-size RoomFeng owners, plus an explicit handoff for users whose required set is specifically bed + desk + wardrobe.
- Added: existing-owner table for 8 × 10 through 12 × 12 ft and one concise intent handoff.
- Intentionally not added: new size permutations, a canonical change, or an aggressive rewrite of the measured prototype.

## 5. `studio apartment layout planner`

- Visible result types: interactive 2D/3D planners and zone-planning guides. Examples reviewed include [RoomSketch3D](https://roomsketch3d.com/use-cases/studio-apartment-planning), [RoomSketcher](https://www.roomsketcher.com/room-planner/), and [FloorPlanGen](https://floorplangen.com/).
- Common coverage: measure walls and fixed features, define sleep/work/eat/lounge zones, place anchor furniture, and use diagrams or interactive plans.
- RoomFeng gap: the existing pillar page had a measured prototype but did not yet connect its generic intent to the five existing square-footage owners or explain why listing area is not the same as unobstructed open floor.
- Added: 300/350/400/450/500 sq ft owner table, listing-area caveat, and geometry contrast for two 400 sq ft rectangles.
- Intentionally not added: intermediate studio URLs, stock imagery, AI interior renders, or a second studio planner.

## 6. `400 sq ft studio apartment layout`

- Visible result types: editorial layout guides, apartment-size explainers, and floor-plan examples. Examples reviewed include [Snug Apartment's 400 sq ft layouts](https://snugapartment.com/studio-apartment-400-sqft-layout/), [Apartment List's size examples](https://www.apartmentlist.com/renter-life/how-big-is-400-square-feet), and [RoomSketch3D's studio planning guide](https://roomsketch3d.com/help/room-planning/studio-apartment-layout).
- Common coverage: long-rectangle examples around 14 × 28 ft, square examples around 20 × 20 ft, zoning, anchor furniture, and floor-plan diagrams.
- RoomFeng gap: the studio pillar did not explicitly show that equal area can produce different circulation and zoning behavior, nor point directly to the existing dedicated 400 sq ft owner.
- Added: 16 × 25 ft versus 20 × 20 ft geometry studies, explicit geometry-only caveat, and contextual link to the existing 400 sq ft page.
- Intentionally not added: a new 400 sq ft URL, a universal furniture list, or a claim that any example is an actual apartment plan.
